Why isn't it on the Play Store?

Xanadu's superpower — operating other apps for you via accessibility — is something store review treats with heavy suspicion regardless of how responsibly it's built. Distributing directly means we can ship the full capability, and you always get the newest build straight from us.

What happens to my data?

Your API key, memories and conversations live only on your phone. The only thing our server ever checks is whether your license is active. Uninstall the app and it's all gone.

Which AI should I use with it?

Any of Claude (Anthropic), GPT (OpenAI) or Gemini (Google) — you paste the key for whichever you have. The Auto setting uses a fast model for chat and automatically switches to a stronger one when Xanadu starts doing real work.

Can it really spend my money?

Only with your fingerprint. Anything irreversible — placing an order, sending an email, confirming a booking — triggers a biometric system prompt describing exactly what's about to happen. No fingerprint, no action.
