Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

Summary: Xanadu Lynx stores your settings, memories, conversation history and connected-service credentials locally on your Android device. Your prompts and attachments are sent only to the AI provider you select, and only to provide the service you request. On our website, Meta's advertising pixel only runs if you accept advertising cookies.

Who we are

Xanadu Lynx (“Xanadu”, “we”, “us”) provides a voice-first Android assistant and the website xanadulynx.com. Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@xanadulynx.com.

Xanadu comes in two versions: a free version on Google Play, and a paid version downloaded from xanadulynx.com that can also operate other apps and make phone calls for you. This policy covers both. Where something applies to only one version, we say so.

Information the app processes

  • Account and licence information (website version): email address, licence token and subscription/entitlement status.
  • User content: prompts, voice transcriptions, images or files you choose to attach, and responses generated for you.
  • App data: settings, saved memories, reminders, conversation history and API keys or tokens you choose to add.
  • Device permissions: microphone, notifications, approximate or precise location, contacts, notification access, display-over-other-apps access and, in the website version only, accessibility. Android asks for permission before access. You can revoke permissions in system settings.
  • Connected-service data: only data needed for an action you request, such as calendar events, email, maps/place results, Spotify data or a contact's phone number.
  • Purchase and diagnostic information: our payment provider processes payment details. We may receive subscription status and basic server logs needed for security, entitlement checks and troubleshooting; we do not receive full card details.

How information is used

We process information to answer requests, operate authorised phone features, remember preferences, schedule reminders, authenticate connected services, verify licences, prevent abuse, provide support and maintain security. We do not sell personal information, and we never use what you say to the app or what it sees on your screen for advertising.

AI providers and third parties

When you submit a request, relevant text, voice transcription or attachment is transmitted to the AI provider you selected (such as Anthropic, OpenAI or Google) using the API credential configured in the app. Their privacy terms apply. Actions you request may also send necessary data to the relevant service, such as Google, Spotify, maps/place providers, email or calling services. Payments are processed by Stripe. Website hosting and server functions are provided by Vercel. If you accept advertising cookies on our website, Meta receives the website data described below.

Phone calls (website version)

If you ask Xanadu to phone someone, the number, what you want from the call and any details you give it are sent to our call server. The call is placed through Twilio, our telephone provider, and OpenAI's voice AI speaks and listens on the call, so the call audio and a live transcript pass through our server, Twilio and OpenAI. We don't record calls or keep transcripts in a database. The transcript is sent back to your phone and held in our server's memory only temporarily, and our server logs keep a partly hidden phone number and a one-line outcome of the call.

Our website, cookies and advertising

  • Hosting: Vercel processes your IP address, browser details and the pages you request in order to deliver and secure the site.
  • Visitor counts: Vercel Web Analytics counts page views, the page that referred you, your country and device type. It does not use cookies and does not identify you.
  • Meta Pixel (only if you accept): if you tap Accept, Meta's pixel loads and records that you visited, which of our pages you viewed, and whether you tapped Download, started checkout or completed a purchase. Meta receives your IP address, browser and device information and those events, and sets cookies (_fbp, _fbc) that last up to 90 days. We use this to measure which of our Facebook and Instagram ads work and to show our ads to people likely to be interested. Meta uses this data under its own Privacy Policy and may link it to your Facebook or Instagram account. If you decline, the pixel never loads.
  • Your choice: we remember Accept or Decline in your browser's local storage, not in a cookie. You can change it at any time, and declining after accepting deletes the Meta cookies we can reach. You can also manage ad settings in your Facebook or Instagram account.
  • Checkout: payment pages are run by Stripe, which may use its own cookies to prevent fraud.

Legal bases

Where UK or EU data-protection law applies, we rely on your consent for advertising cookies; on our contract with you to provide a licence and the features you ask for; and on our legitimate interests in running, securing and improving the service, counting visitors and providing support.

International transfers

Our suppliers, including Vercel, Stripe, Meta and the AI provider you choose, may process data outside the UK, for example in the United States, under their own legal safeguards for international transfers.

Storage, security and retention

App settings, memories, conversation history and connected-service credentials are stored locally on your device in app-private storage. Network connections use HTTPS. Licence and service servers retain only information needed to operate, secure and support the service or meet legal obligations. Local app data remains until you delete it, clear app storage or uninstall the app. You can remove individual connected-service credentials in the app's settings.

Accessibility and sensitive permissions

In the website version, if enabled, Android's Accessibility Service lets Xanadu read visible screen content and perform taps, typing and scrolling solely to carry out tasks you request. Accessibility data is not used for advertising and is not sold. Microphone access supports voice input. Location supports “near me”, places and route requests. Contacts access resolves a name to contact details when you ask to call or message someone. These permissions are optional and can be disabled at any time.

Your choices and rights

You can decline or revoke Android permissions, disconnect services, delete local conversations and memories by clearing app data or uninstalling, change your cookie choice using the button above, and ask us to access, correct or delete server-held personal information by emailing hello@xanadulynx.com. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to object, restrict processing or complain to a data-protection authority; in the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

Xanadu is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app and website change. The latest version and effective date will always appear on this page.
